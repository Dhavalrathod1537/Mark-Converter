Quick Reference

CGPA Scale

  • 9.5-10.0 = 90-100%
  • 8.5-9.4 = 80-89%
  • 7.5-8.4 = 70-79%
  • 6.5-7.4 = 60-69%
  • 5.5-6.4 = 50-59%

Grade Points

  • O = 10 (90-100%)
  • A+ = 9 (80-89%)
  • A = 8 (70-79%)
  • B+ = 7 (60-69%)
  • B = 6 (50-59%)
